The craft

Ajrakh is a block printing tradition from Kutch and Sindh. Every metre is printed by hand with carved wooden blocks, then dyed in indigo and madder root, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

One cloth goes through the printer's hands many times over several days. Nothing is rushed, because the dye needs time to set.

  • Wash and prepare. The cloth is washed and soaked so it takes dye evenly.
  • Block print. Resist paste and mordants are stamped on with carved blocks.
  • Dye. The cloth is dipped in indigo and boiled with madder for the deep reds.
  • Wash and dry. It is rinsed in running water and dried in the sun.
